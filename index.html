CollegeDirectorate of Higher Education

Free Professional Education Scheme for Girls — Higher Education

The MahaDBT scheme details describe support for minority students pursuing recognized courses after Class 12.

  • Maharashtra resident; HSC passed from Maharashtra.
  • Parents’ annual income: ₹8 lakh or less.
  • Cannot receive another scholarship or stipend under this scheme.
More eligibility & documents

Applies to minority students in the first through final year of recognized Arts, Commerce, and Science courses after Class 12. Renewal requires admission to the next academic year. The portal states that 30% of scholarships are reserved for female students. Incorrect information can result in cancellation.

Documents listed: domicile certificate, previous year’s marksheet, and bonafide certificate.

Timeline / benefit amount: not stated in the published scheme detail. Approving authority: Higher and Technical Education. Grievance contact: college Principal, Regional Officer, or DHE Administrative Officer.

CollegeDirectorate of Higher Education

Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Tuition Fee Scholarship — SEBC

The published scheme description refers to higher education support for students from Economically Weaker Sections.

  • Maharashtra residents; students from the Maharashtra–Karnataka border area may also apply.
  • Parents’ annual income: ₹8 lakh or less.
  • Only the first two children in a family; no other scholarship benefit.
More eligibility & documents

Students must be admitted to courses specified in the DHE Government Resolutions dated 07/10/2017 and 10/10/2024. Distance, open, virtual, and part-time courses are excluded. The course must have required government and regulatory approval, and students must appear for each semester or academic-year examination.

Benefits listed: examination fee and tuition fee. Documents listed: domicile certificate, previous year’s marksheet, and bonafide certificate.

Students who received this scholarship in the previous year may apply for renewal. The published detail does not state an application timeline or approving authority. Grievance contact: college Principal, Regional Officer, or DHE Administrative Officer.

College · Hostel supportDirectorate of Higher Education

Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Hostel Maintenance Allowance — SEBC

The MahaDBT description lists hostel maintenance support for students from Economically Weaker Sections.

  • Requires a Maharashtra domicile certificate.
  • Parent farmer / registered labourer certificate, or an income certificate for the preceding financial year.
  • Two-child family declaration is listed as required.
More eligibility & documents

CAP-related documents apply only to Law, Education, and Physical Education courses. Gap-related documents are needed if there is an academic gap.

Benefits listed: food, accommodation, and subsistence allowances. Documents listed: domicile certificate, previous year’s marksheet, and bonafide certificate.

Approving authority: Higher and Technical Education. Grievance contact: college Principal, Regional Officer, or DHE Administrative Officer. No application timeline or income threshold is stated in the published detail.
